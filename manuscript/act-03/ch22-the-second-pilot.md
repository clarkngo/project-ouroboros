# Chapter 22 — The Second Pilot

**Synopsis:** Claire returns to the financial-services account from Ch. 11–13 (and 17's near-departure) to relaunch on the new harness, under the deadline established in Ch. 18. Structural mirror of Ch. 6's Vantage Rail launch, but higher stakes and a materially more mature product — the chapter should let the reader feel how much has actually changed since Ch. 6 without the narration stating it outright.

**Key beats:**
- Claire pitches using the fully-formed version of "tiered honesty" (Ch. 2's seed, Ch. 6's first real use): a specific, bounded SLA built directly from eval pass-rate data, with the circuit-breaker and exception-cache architecture explained to a skeptical, still-burned customer as the actual answer to "what happens when it's wrong," not a hand-wave.
- Tension: this customer, unlike Marisol in Ch. 6, has already been burned (Ch. 17) and is not inclined to extend trust easily — Claire has to earn it back specifically, not generically.
- A structural echo of Ch. 6's "show me the failure case" beat, but this time the team can show a live, complete incident lifecycle — detection, circuit-breaker halt, human-in-the-loop escalation, resolution — end to end, because the full harness now exists.
- Ends with a provisional yes — real, but conditional, withholding the full unambiguous "win" for Ch. 23's actual test.
