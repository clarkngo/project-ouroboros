# Chapter 20 — The Harness

**Synopsis:** The build-out chapter — Devon's arc reaches its practical turning point as he moves from single hand-patcher to architect of a system designed explicitly so no one person, including him, can be a silent SPOF again. Focuses on the concrete construction of schema-validated tool interfaces and versioned, reviewed prompts (real code review, real regression gates, no more nine-minute unilateral merges).

**Key beats:**
- A deliberate mirror of Ch. 3: Devon opens a prompt file again, but this time the change goes through the full pipeline — schema validation, eval regression run, a second reviewer who can meaningfully evaluate it because the eval suite makes the change's effects legible instead of requiring tribal knowledge to assess.
- Emotional beat: Devon initially finds the new process slow and mourns the old velocity, then experiences a moment (possibly a near-miss caught cleanly by the new gate) that converts him fully — the harness catches something he would have missed, proving its worth on his own terms rather than as an imposed discipline.
- Onboards a second engineer onto the agent runtime for the first time in the book's timeline — a small, concrete image of Devon no longer being irreplaceable, played for quiet relief rather than melodrama.
- Ends with Devon, for the first time in the book, taking a full weekend off without being paged — a small, earned, understated payoff.
