# Chapter 11 — The Quarterly Number

**Synopsis:** Claire's arc regresses under real pressure — the discipline she embraced in Act I collides with a board that wants growth on the old timeline regardless of what she now understands about the tail risk. A new, larger prospective account (a financial-services company, higher stakes than Vantage Rail or Halden) wants autonomy levels beyond what the eval suite and tool-scoping work currently support, and offers a contract large enough to change Meridian's year.

**Key beats:**
- Claire is caught between the board's renewed growth pressure (callback to Ch. 2's Harmon) and her own new standard for what she's willing to promise; the chapter should not let her off easy — she seriously considers overselling again, rationalizing it as "the eval suite will catch up before launch."
- Direct confrontation with Alex, who is now the one holding the line Claire held in Ch. 2 — a deliberate mirroring of their Ch. 2 dynamic with positions partially reversed.
- Claire commits to an aggressive but not yet reckless timeline — sets up the compressed schedule that produces the pressure behind Ch. 12's crisis and Ch. 17's outage.
- Ends on Claire alone, doing the math on her own comp and credibility against what she actually now believes is safe — the chapter's job is to make her genuinely torn, not villainous.
