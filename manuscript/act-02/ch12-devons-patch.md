# Chapter 12 — Devon's Patch

**Synopsis:** The compressed timeline from Ch. 11 lands squarely on Devon. Under deadline pressure, he reverts to the old habit — a fast, unreviewed, late-night hand-patch to meet the new financial-services account's requirements — bypassing the very eval-suite discipline he spent Ch. 9 building. The chapter's tension is entirely earned by how understandable the lapse is: the eval suite isn't fast enough yet to fit inside the compressed timeline, and Devon, exhausted and still the only person who truly understands the full prompt file, takes the shortcut he knows better than to take.

**Key beats:**
- Show the emotional pull of being needed — the chapter should let Devon feel *good* in the moment of the fast patch, a deliberate callback to Ch. 3's "private, unshared satisfaction," making clear the pattern is a genuine temptation, not just a process failure.
- The patch works, initially — no immediate incident — which makes it worse: it reinforces the old habit rather than punishing it, and nobody catches that it bypassed the gate.
- A quiet, unresolved beat where Devon considers telling Alex he skipped the eval suite, and doesn't, rationalizing that it'll get backfilled later. This omission becomes load-bearing for Ch. 17.
- Chapter closes on Devon, alone, aware at some level of what he's just done and choosing not to examine it too closely — sets up his Ch. 14/18 reckoning.
