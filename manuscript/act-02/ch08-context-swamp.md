# Chapter 8 — Context Swamp

**Synopsis:** Opens Act II directly off the Halden expansion demo's cliffhanger. Alex and Devon attempt to audit the full scope of stale/contradictory context across the knowledge base and accreted prompts, and discover the problem is bigger and less tractable than either expected — not a finite list of stale documents to fix, but an ongoing structural condition: anything that goes into the shared scratchpad or knowledge base decays in *relevance* even when it doesn't decay in *storage*, and nothing currently distinguishes the two. Introduce the mechanics of the scratchpad's token-ceiling eviction policy (none, beyond a hard cutoff) as the proximate mechanism. First appearance of the phrase "invisible debt" used explicitly by a character (Alex, in a conversation with Claire) as the chapter's thematic anchor.

**Key beats:**
- Cold open: a second, smaller context-swamp incident (different customer, different symptom — perhaps a contradictory instruction from two different eras of a prompt file both technically still "in scope") lands while the Halden audit is still underway, establishing this as systemic rather than isolated.
- Devon and Alex build a crude first-pass audit tool and are alarmed by what it surfaces.
- Claire, newly reading the incident digest closely (per Ch. 6/7's turn), asks the question that reframes the chapter's stakes commercially: "If this is structural, what do I tell customers about the ones we haven't found yet?"
- Ends on Alex deciding to formally request the eval-suite headcount/roadmap slot she promised Claire in Ch. 6, now with real urgency instead of a theoretical case.
