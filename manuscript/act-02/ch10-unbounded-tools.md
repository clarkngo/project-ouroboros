# Chapter 10 — Unbounded Tools

**Synopsis:** First Sloan POV chapter — widens the aperture into her working life and the scale of what she's actually tracking across the company, beyond the single Vantage Rail negotiation seen in Ch. 5. As Meridian's agent products expand into more customer accounts, the tool surface has grown faster than governance has: dozens of integrations, inconsistent scoping, several discovered via Sloan's own audit rather than reported by the teams that built them ("shadow tools"). Establishes the scale of the unconstrained-tool-use problem as a company-wide condition, not a one-deal negotiation.

**Key beats:**
- Sloan runs an internal red-team exercise against a tool integration nobody flagged for review, and it goes further than expected — not catastrophic, but far enough to be a serious wake-up call, shown from her clinical, procedural POV rather than as melodrama.
- Friction with a platform team lead (not Devon — a new or minor character) who built a convenience integration without going through Sloan's process, establishing that the Ch. 5 resolution with Claire hasn't fully propagated into company-wide practice yet.
- Sloan brings the finding to Alex directly, bypassing the usual review cadence, which begins pulling Alex further into direct partnership with Sloan (sets up their alliance deepening across Act II).
- Thematic beat: Sloan articulates, for the reader, the least-privilege principle explicitly, and the chapter closes on her starting to design what will become the schema-validated tool interface system realized in Act III.
