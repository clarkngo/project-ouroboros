# Chapter 13 — The Injection

**Synopsis:** The vindication chapter for Sloan's Act I warnings (Ch. 5, Ch. 10) — a real prompt-injection incident hits the new financial-services account, exploiting a gap adjacent to (though not identical to) the shortcut Devon took in Ch. 12. An adversarial actor embeds instruction-shaped text in a document the agent processes, attempting to exfiltrate account data through a tool call. Unlike the Ch. 1 runaway loop, this is an external, adversarial failure rather than an internal reasoning failure — a deliberately different flavor of incident to keep Act II's crises from feeling repetitive.

**Key beats:**
- Because of Sloan's Ch. 10 groundwork (least-privilege scoping already partially rolled out) the exfiltration attempt is contained — scoped credentials mean the compromised tool call can't reach the full blast radius it would have reached in Ch. 5's original architecture. The chapter should make clear this is a genuine partial win for the disciplines built so far, not a total catastrophe, to avoid Act II reading as pure doom.
- However, the incident review surfaces that Devon's Ch. 12 patch bypassed the eval suite, and while not the direct cause of this particular incident, it reveals the process gap has been silently normalized — raising the question of what else has gone unvetted.
- Sloan and Alex, now firmly allied, present a unified, clinical incident report; Claire has to take it to the financial-services customer directly, testing the "tiered honesty" sales approach from Ch. 2/6 under much higher stakes than Vantage Rail.
- Sets up Ch. 14's postmortem and the political fallout between Claire and Alex.
